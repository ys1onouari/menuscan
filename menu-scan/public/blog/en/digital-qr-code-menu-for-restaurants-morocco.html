Digital QR Code menu for restaurants: the complete guide for Morocco

2 February 2026 · 6 min

Pricing, hosting, setup and best practices for a digital QR Code menu in a Moroccan restaurant.

A printed menu gets damaged, has to be reprinted, and is never up to date. A digital QR Code menu is fixed with a single message and opens on any phone, with no app to install. For a restaurant, café or snack bar in Morocco, it is the simplest way to move from paper to a menu that is always current.

Why switch to a digital menu

The QR Code replaces the poster or the printed menu without removing anything from the table. Customers scan with the device they already have in their hand, the menu opens in their browser, and you keep control of your prices.

Concretely, it solves three immediate problems:

  • price or dish changes need no reprinting;
  • the menu stays readable even when the card is exposed to sunlight or dust;
  • the same code can be shared across tables, the window and social media.
